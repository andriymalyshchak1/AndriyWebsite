Meet Eduardo

Eduardo Duran is a versatile real estate professional with a background in engineering, computer science, and real estate investment strategies. Originally from El Paso, Texas, he moved to Austin to study Mechanical Engineering at the University of Texas at Austin, where he also explored computer science and commercial real estate finance.

Eduardo’s real estate journey began at 19 in the UT Austin market as a leasing agent, where he has since facilitated hundreds of leases, consistently delivering top results for landlords and tenants. His expertise spans leasing, real estate investments, property flips, and BRRRR strategies, with a strong understanding of DSCR products to support clients in complex financial decisions. Fluent in English and Spanish, Eduardo is committed to providing exceptional service with integrity.

Outside of work, he enjoys traveling, perfecting steak-cooking, playing basketball, and attending comedy shows, adding a well-rounded perspective to his real estate practice.
